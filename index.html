About

I'm a semanticist working on the interface between syntax and semantics. Most of my research is about intensionality: how noun phrases come to quantify over individual concepts (as in Most governors have changed after the election), how plurality and intensionality interact, how wh-questions interact with de re and de dicto readings, and what person, number and gender features contribute in intensional contexts. Other interests include comparatives and degree constructions, reciprocals and distributivity, and Brazilian Portuguese.

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Salzburg. Before that, I was a lecturer in syntax and semantics at the University of Pennsylvania (2023–2024). I received my PhD from MIT in 2023, with a dissertation on quantification over individual concepts.
